# Code of Conduct

Joshua+ is made by musicians for musicians, of every level, genre, language and background. We want this to be a friendly place to play.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version:

## Do

- Be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to beginners.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and credit others' work.

## Don't

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Make discriminatory jokes or attacks based on gender, identity, orientation, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, experience or anything else.
- Publish other people's private information.
- Share pirated music or copyrighted audio in issues, pull requests or test fixtures.

## Enforcement

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile [@fabandrade88](https://github.com/fabandrade88), or through a private security advisory if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
