## You may, without asking

- Say that your project is **based on**, a **fork of** or **compatible with** Joshua+, and link to this repository.
- Use the name to refer to Joshua+ itself, for example in a review, a tutorial or a news article.
- Build and run Joshua+ from this repository for yourself, unchanged, under its own name.

## Please ask first

- Publishing, selling or distributing a modified version, or a build you made yourself, under the name **Joshua+** or a confusingly similar name (such as "Joshua Plus" or "JoshuaPlus Pro").
- Using the logo or app icon, or a confusingly similar one, for another product, service, website or account.
- Anything that suggests that your project, company or release is the official Joshua+ or is endorsed by its author.

A fork is welcome: give it its own name and icon, keep the MIT copyright notice, and say where it comes from.
